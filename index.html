Process

How it works

Five steps toshowtime.

From the first call to the last cue, one team carries your event through five clear stages.

01Day 1–3

Discover

We visit the venue, understand your guests and goals, and turn it all into a clear one-page brief.

  • Venue recce
  • Guest & budget plan
  • Brief in 72 hours
02Week 1

Design

Concept, mood and a 3D walkthrough of your stage and décor, with an itemised quote to match.

  • Concept deck
  • 3D walkthrough
  • Itemised quote
03Weeks 2–6

Build

Sets and décor are fabricated, artists booked and permits filed, then everything is tested before it travels.

  • Fabrication
  • Artist bookings
  • Permits & safety
04Event week

Rig

Our crew builds on site: sound, light, LED and staging, followed by a full technical rehearsal.

  • On-site build
  • Sound & light check
  • Full rehearsal
05The day

Showtime

One director calls every cue live while you enjoy the night. We wrap and clear the venue after.

  • Live cue-calling
  • Guest flow
  • Clean wrap
